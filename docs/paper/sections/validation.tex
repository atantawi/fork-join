\label{sec:validation}

Key observations:
\begin{itemize}
\item The approximation is \textbf{exact} for the homogeneous case (errors $< 0.05$\% at low/moderate load, $< 0.5$\% at high load due to simulation noise).
\item For heterogeneous cases, the error is consistently \textbf{positive} (slight overestimation), staying within $+2$\% across all tested scenarios.
\item The largest error occurs at moderate heterogeneity ($\mu_2/\mu_1 \approx 1.5$) under heavy load ($\rho_1 = 0.9$), reaching $+1.8$\%.
\item For strong heterogeneity ($\mu_2/\mu_1 \geq 3$), errors are small because $\Tub$ and $\Tbot$ converge (the bottleneck dominates).
\end{itemize}
