\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

For a heterogeneous two-queue M/M/1 fork-join system with service rates $\mu_1$, $\mu_2$ and Poisson arrival rate~$\lambda$, the mean response time is well approximated by:
\begin{equation}
T \;\approx\; \left(1 - \frac{\rho_1 + \rho_2}{8}\right) \Tub \;+\; \frac{\rho_1 + \rho_2}{8}\; \Tbot,
\end{equation}
where $\Tub$ and $\Tbot$ are defined in~\eqref{eq:tub} and~\eqref{eq:tbot}. This expression is exact for homogeneous servers, satisfies known bounds, and agrees with simulation to within 2\% across a wide range of parameters.
