\begin{abstract}
We derive a simple closed-form approximation for the mean response time of a heterogeneous two-queue M/M/1 fork-join system, where servers may have different service rates. The approximation interpolates between an independent upper bound and a bottleneck lower bound using a weight determined by the average server utilization. The formula is exact for the homogeneous case (recovering the Nelson--Tantawi result), satisfies known bounds, and exhibits correct limiting behavior in both light and heavy traffic. Discrete-event simulation validates the approximation to within 2\% across a wide range of parameters.
\end{abstract}
